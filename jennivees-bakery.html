Our story

Built with love, for everyone.

Jennivee's opened its doors in 2017 on one simple belief: everyone deserves cake. It's Chicago's queer-owned bakery and café, and that welcome runs through everything — the everyday case full of gourmet cupcakes and layer cakes, the Filipino-inspired flavors like ube and buko pandan, and the specialty Pride cakes the shop bakes every June.

It's a small space with a big personality — regulars come for the coffee and stay for the people-watching as much as the pastries.
